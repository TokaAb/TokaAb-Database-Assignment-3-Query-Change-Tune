We should use Numbers (INT or BIGINT) for normal database tables because they use less storage and provide better performance for indexing and searching.

We should use GUIDs (UNIQUEIDENTIFIER) when we work with multiple databases or distributed systems because they can be generated independently and reduce ID conflicts.

The main disadvantage of GUIDs is that they use more storage and can make indexes larger and slower.

**Conclusion: Numbers are better for performance and storage, while GUIDs are better for distributed systems and global uniqueness.
